## The shape of the system

Freight quoting used to mean a person reading an inbound shipping request, working out a price, checking the relevant compliance constraints, and writing back to the customer.
The agentic system automates that pipeline with four cooperating sub-agents, and the division of labor is the point:

- A language model parses the inbound request into structured shipment facts - the one job it is genuinely best at.
- Deterministic, tested code computes every quote.
  No price is ever generated by a model.
- A retrieval agent cross-checks the quote against local compliance documentation before anything moves forward.
- A drafting agent writes the customer-facing reply, and a human reviewer approves or rejects every single output in a review console before it is sent.

Nothing reaches a customer without a person signing off.
That human gate is not a temporary training wheel; it is the disposition model the system is designed around.

## Why it matters

This was the engagement that convinced me the trustworthy-agent pattern generalizes: language models at the language boundaries, tested code owning the numbers, retrieval grounding the claims, and a human owning the send button.
The same skeleton shows up in my public work - the trade-compliance agent and the travel approval system on this site are the inspectable versions of it.
